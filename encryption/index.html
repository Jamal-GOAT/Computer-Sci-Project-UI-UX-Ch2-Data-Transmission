What is Encryption?

Encryption is the process of converting readable data, called plaintext, into an unreadable form called ciphertext. A key is used with an encryption algorithm to transform the data, helping prevent unauthorised people from simply reading it.

Plaintext & Ciphertext

Plaintext is readable data before encryption. After encryption, it becomes ciphertext, which is unreadable without the correct key.
