Transmission Methods

Data can be transmitted between devices in different ways depending on how the data is sent and how devices communicate.

Serial

Parallel

Serial Transmission

Serial transmission sends data one bit at a time through a single communication channel. The bits are sent sequentially, one after another, until the complete data is transmitted. It is simpler and cheaper to implement, and it is commonly used in devices such as USB connections.

Parallel Transmission

Parallel transmission sends multiple bits at the same time through several communication channels. Each bit can travel throguh its own wire, allowing a group of bits to be transmitted at once. It is generally quicker but can be expensive and harder to implement. It is used mostly in CPU data and address buses and RAM memory buses.
